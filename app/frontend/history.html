History

MRSAC is a well-established premier institution and recognized as one of the best State Centre in Natural Resource Monitoring and Management by offering innovative, effective and optimal solutions by blending RS and GIS technologies with the known technique. Maharashtra Remote Sensing Application Centre (M.R.S.A.C) was founded in September 1988 under the administrative control of Planning Department, Government of Maharashtra. It was registered under the Societies Register Act, 1860.

The Centre's matter is managed by the Governing Body with its members appointed by GoM and assisted by Executive committee constituted by Governing Body. At the inception time, the Centre initiated its work in small laboratory in the premises of Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT formerly VRCE) campus, Nagpur, with limited Infrastructure, Instrumentation and Skilled manpower.

MRSAC started its journey with four Scientists, four Technical and five Supporting staff, mostly on deputation. From August 1994, the Centre started functioning from its new office building from the same VNIT premises with increased Scientific, Technical and Administrative staff.

The Centre is keeping in pace with cutting-edge technology in the field of RS-GIS, GPS, 3D-technology, Radar, Lidar, Web-GIS, Cloud Computing. Now the Centre has highly specialized dedicated computer labs as 3D-Photogrammetry lab, Drone data processing Lab, Web-portal and web-site generation lab.

The Centre has spread its wings by establishing two new branch offices at Mumbai (2013) to keep liaising with GoM and Pune (2014). MRSAC is a "Nodal Agency" for developing a Maharashtra Geo-Spatial Digital Database System (MGDDS) for Govt. of Maharashtra and works in coordination with Planning Department, GoM.

The Geo-Portal was inaugurated on 20th December 2011 by the Honorable Chief Minister of Maharashtra State. MRSAC re-affirms its commitment towards a better, transparent, vibrant and implementable E-governed Maharashtra. Serving the State through RS-GIS digitally — A move to Digital India Initiative.
